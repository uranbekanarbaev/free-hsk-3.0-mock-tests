# Sources and edition

The exam papers, answer keys and recordings come from the supplied **HSK3.0** sample package. The [official HSK page](https://www.chinesetest.cn/HSK) links the [HSK 3.0 sample ZIP](https://hsk-sample-1409982902.cos.ap-beijing.myqcloud.com/HSK3.0.zip) from its HSK 3.0 navigation. Checked 28 September 2026.

The papers identify the **Center for Language Education and Cooperation (中外语言交流合作中心)** as their producer. hsk-tutor.com organises the materials for learners and adds instructions, blank practice sheets and offline start pages; it does not claim authorship of the examination content or official affiliation.

## What changed?

- The original PDFs are copied byte for byte, with shorter English filenames.
- Audio retains the entire recording and sequence, re-encoded to **64 kbps mono MP3 at 24 kHz** for smaller downloads. Source and output durations are checked to differ by less than 0.2 seconds. For the original audio quality, use the official ZIP above.
- Paper timings and question counts are transcribed from the supplied covers; separate speaking times for levels 3–6 come from their speaking-paper covers.
- Practice answer sheets and review logs are original learner aids, not official answer cards.
- HSK 7–9 uses a shared sample paper; its translation prompts in this package are in English.

The file index at [tests/files.json](tests/files.json) maps each distributed original asset to its source filename and SHA-256. Audio entries also record the original file hash and the output duration. These are identification details, not claims that the official provider has approved this edition.

The collection contains one sample paper for each of HSK 1–6 and one shared sample for HSK 7–9, plus speaking materials for levels 3–6. It contains no HSK 2.0 papers. Exam content retains its source rights: [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
